Battledome Autofighter Planner

Tell the Autofighter exactly what to use on each turn, without fiddling with the turn numbers yourself.

In the Battledome, click Download on the Autofighter's toolbar. You get a file called battledome.settings.json. Or click Copy (version 3.3.3 and up) and paste the text below.

Or paste the copied text instead

🔒 Everything stays in your browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere. Since version 3.3.2 the Autofighter leaves your SDB PIN out of this file. If yours is older, the file may include it, so don't share it.

Presets

On the left, what the preset does right now. On the right, your plan: it starts as a copy, so just click a slot to change it. The two weapons can go in any order, and the last round repeats once your list runs out.

Assumes every weapon in the preset is equipped to the pet that fights. Weapons and abilities that are used up (once per battle, single use) or cooling down are skipped, like the Autofighter does. Weapons that "may break" break at random, so they always show as usable. Smart healing isn't shown.

Back in the Battledome, click Upload on the Autofighter's toolbar and pick the file you just downloaded. If you used Copy as text, click Paste instead (version 3.3.3 and up). Only this preset changes, the rest of your settings stay the same.
